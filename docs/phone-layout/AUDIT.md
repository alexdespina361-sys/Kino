# Phone layout: audit of what is there today

Taken on `origin/main` = `91bd180` (before any change of this branch), in Chromium with touch emulation, with the made-up catalogue (no real TMDB, no real streams).
Research the numbers are judged against: `RESEARCH.md` (rules **R1..R15**). What is built to fix it: `PLAN.md`.

## What "the phone" is: two paths

Kino has one page and two screens, picked by `src/client/role.ts`. A phone meets **both**:

| Path | What the phone shows | How it is reached | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. The remote** | the touch remote: pick something, press play, a TV does the playing | any touch device that opens the site | `src/client/controller/*`, `polish/phone.css` |
| **B. The phone as the screen** | **the TV page itself** (left rail, hero, wide rows, TV player), shrunk onto the phone | `/tv`, `?role=tv`, a party link (`?party=`), "Use this phone as the screen" | `src/client/tv/*`, `tv/tv.css`, `polish/tv.css` |

The brief's complaint ("it's just the TV version weirdly sized for the phone") is literally path B, and in spirit also path A (a TV-shaped page in a column). Both are audited and both get a phone layout.

## How it was measured (and what the numbers can and cannot say)

* `scripts/ui-walk/run.mjs --scan` walks the app and, for every picture, `scripts/ui-walk/scan.mjs` records every element's live computed style and box. `scripts/ui-walk/phone-numbers.mjs` turns those into the numbers below. Both are part of this branch.
* Six sizes (CSS px): **320x568, 360x800, 390x844, 412x915** (phones), **844x390** (a phone held sideways), **768x1024** (a small tablet, which gets the remote too). Remote walked as guest and signed in; path B walked as guest.
* **On screen** = in the viewport and not under something else (the scan asks the browser what is on top of the middle of each box). When a sheet is open only what is *inside* the sheet counts (what is behind the scrim cannot be touched).
* **Smallest text** = the smallest computed font size of any visible text box or field. The sample line of the subtitle-style preview (`.cap-sample`) is left out on purpose: it is a scaled picture of what the TV shows, not page text (it measures 9.3 px at 390 and 7.5 px at 320).
* **Smallest target** = the smaller side of the larger of a control's drawing and its `::before/::after` hit area. 44 px is the floor (Apple 44, NN/g 1 cm, M3 48 dp, R10); WCAG 2.5.8's 24 px is only the legal minimum.
* **Sheet cover** = the part of the screen height a bottom sheet fills.
* Not measured: anything a real device adds (see "Not verified" at the end).

Reproduce (from the worktree root; the server is started by the runner, `--port` must be free):

```
$env:SCAN_OUT='.ui-polish/scan/before'
node scripts/ui-walk/run.mjs --only=phone,phone-tv --scan --size=390x844 --out=.ui-polish/shots/before --port=8831   # repeat per --size
node scripts/ui-walk/phone-numbers.mjs .ui-polish/scan/before phone-390 phone-390-signed-in screen-390x844 --detail
```

Pictures: `.ui-polish/shots/before/phone-<size>[-signed-in]/` (remote) and `.ui-polish/shots/before/screen-<size>/` (path B); git-ignored, regenerated by the command above. Names below are those files.

## Where the causes are (for the plan)

* Remote (A1-A6, A10): `Controller.tsx` puts everything in `section.home` in one column and opens every secondary thing as a `Sheet` (`Sheets.tsx`, `TitleSheet.tsx`, `AccountScreen.tsx`'s `Page`). Search, filters and rows are the `Library` component at the bottom of that column. Sheet height is `max-height: min(86vh, calc(100% - 8px))` in `polish/phone.css`.
* Phone as the screen (B1-B9): the same `Tv` / `Browse` / `Rail` / `Detail` / `Hud` components as a TV, sized from a viewport-based `--em`; `thisIsPhone()` is only used to hide a few things (`Browse.tsx`) and to move Back.
* Both: `thisIsPhone()` (touch + coarse pointer + short side under 600) already separates a hand-held from a TV or a big touch screen; tablets get the remote by touch but are not "phone".

## Not verified (everything above)

* **No real phone, no iPhone, no Safari, no real Fire TV.** Everything is Chromium (desktop build) with touch emulation. Android's own font scale ("larger text"), the system gesture bar and notch (`env(safe-area-inset-*)` is 0 in the emulator), the soft keyboard, real touch latency and one-handed reach were **not** measured.
* Only the made-up catalogue and drawn posters were used; a real portrait poster with a real backdrop may crop differently.
* The six sizes are a sample of devices, not a device lab. Contrast of the six accent colours was not re-measured in this audit (done with the build, see `QA.md`).
