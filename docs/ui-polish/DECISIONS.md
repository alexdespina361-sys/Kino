| D5 | **Bug fixes go into both looks** (a dead end, a wrong label, a lost state, an accessibility failure). **Layout, new buttons, changed paths and every purely visual change go only into polish.** | Me (assumed; told to the owner) | Classic must stay what it is for comparison, but nobody wants a known bug kept for reference. If the owner wants classic frozen completely, bug fixes move behind the look switch too: one-line change of policy. |
| D6 | The brand red (`#e50914`) and the six per-profile accents (red, blue, purple, green, orange, pink) do not change. Everything is checked with all six. | Me (assumed; told to the owner) | The plan speaks of "the blue accent": in the app the accent is a per-profile choice, red by default. |
| D7 | No Storybook, no Tailwind, no token framework. A small dev-only lab page (built only on request, never in the production bundle) shows the primitives under both looks and all accents. | Me | Plain CSS app, one icon file, components that depend on page context; Storybook would be a second build for little gain. |
| D8 | Screenshots are the evidence: a walk of every screen on a made-up catalogue with drawn posters (no real titles), the same pictures for either look; baselines are **never** updated automatically. Anything random on a screen (codes, QR pattern, film frame) is pinned for the picture. | Me | Comparable, repeatable, and no real content in the repo. |
| D9 | Audits are eight consolidated, parallel, read-only audits (the plan's nine, with A+D, B+C, G+I paired) plus two user-flow audits (TV, phone) that the plan does not have but the owner's goal ("fix bugs, adding missing buttons, optimizing pathing") needs. Every finding must cite evidence; behaviour bugs need exact repro steps. | Me | Scale: one reviewer per question beats nine thin ones. |
| D10 | Tests stay targeted while working (the specs for what changed); one full e2e run per look at the end, not per wave. | Owner (earlier feedback) | "An hour of tests is too slow". |
| D11 | Figma and the other design-plugin connectors need the owner's authorisation; they are optional and noted in `REGISTRATION_BLOCKERS.md`. Work does not wait for them. | Me | Cannot start an OAuth flow myself. |
| D12 | **TV focus language (refined)**: three shapes, one signal each, all with the same white ring and page-coloured gap. Anything that is a *button for the remote* (button, chip, key, score, action, a settings switch row, a party card) turns light (white fill, dark words) and lifts a little; a *primary action* (Play, Resume, the red button) keeps its accent in the ring (drawn 1.5 times as thick so it stays findable), so it does not lose its colour; a *picture* (tile, card) gets the ring and the lift and keeps its picture; a *line of a list or a menu* (a menu option, an episode row, a line of the bell) goes one step lighter (dark grey, not white) with a thick white ring drawn inside it, so a long list does not turn into a stack of white slabs. The round play button gets the heaviest ring. What is *chosen* is shown by the accent as a tint and ring, never as a solid fill (a solid accent fill means "press this"); a chosen thing the remote is on shows both (light, with the accent ring kept inside it). | Me | One signal for "the remote is here" that stays findable from the sofa on every surface, and the accent keeps one meaning per shape. The first draft ("everything turns white") only tied the classic look in the focus jury, so each shape got its own treatment (`TV_TESTS.md`, section 4). Blind focus juries (refined / classic / tie): all-light draft 8/7/21, three shapes 25/7/8, then the switch row and the party card lost 3 of 3 critics as dark rows and were made light like buttons; round 4 (one of two critics decoded when this was written) has both slightly ahead of classic, Settings slightly behind (classic's thicker ring on the primary button), the player menu a tie. The second critic's result is still to be saved and decoded (`.ui-polish/jury-keys/focus4-result-2.json`). Whole screens: 37/3/5. Phone, portrait 25/12/26 then 30/5/10; landscape 32/0/7. |
| D13 | **TV safe zone (refined)**: controls keep 5% in from the right edge (`--edge-r`; the top bar's corner buttons, a title page's Close, the player's controls and times), the menu rail 2.6% from the left, rows run on to 4% (`--page-r`). | Me | A set with overscan crops 2-5%; the classic look keeps its controls 4.4% in, and the first refined draft (2.6%) was worse than that. |
| D14 | **One corner-button look (refined)**: the round buttons in a picture's corner are all the same dark disc; the glyph says which (＋ keeps, × lets go or clears), not a colour. | Me | A red disc for "kept" next to a dark one for "remove" read as two kinds of thing. |
| D15 | A control's name, drawn above it while the remote is on an icon-only control in the player, is drawn above the progress row (**both looks**): it used to cover the elapsed or remaining time. | Me (bug fix, D5) | With the remote on Play, the elapsed time could not be read. |
| D16 | **Phone type scale (refined)**: six sizes, 13 / 14 / 16 / 18 / 24 / 32 px (`--p-fs-1..6`), three radii (8 / 12 / 22 px), one label colour (`--p-label`). Nothing under 13 px. | Me | Blind jury, phone portrait refined / classic / tie: 25/12/26, then 30/5/10 after the scale was applied. |
| D17 | **Phone, held sideways (refined)**: the page keeps its sheet surfaces and the same scale; targets stay 44 px tall. | Me | Jury, landscape 32/0/7. |
