## 1a. The phone held sideways as a layout of its own (the owner's priority)

**Verdict.** The classic phone UI has no landscape layout. It is a 520px portrait column (`controller.css:2-10`, centred, `.page-body` the same at `account.css:93`) placed in a window that is 800-932 wide and 360-430 tall, with rules only for height (`:11` max-height 700, `:97` max-height 740) and one for width (`:235`, 380); nothing asks for orientation or aspect. What is 2.5 screens tall in portrait becomes 5 screens tall, and the things people reach for (Play, the remote's buttons, a menu's rows, Connect) end up below the fold, while 39-48% of the width stays empty. Everything else in this report is smaller than this.

**1a.4 Principles for a landscape layout (aim: whoever writes the phone's sideways layout; plain CSS, the phone runs a modern browser but keep the TV page rules for anything in `tv.css`).**
1. Decide on height, not width: `@media (orientation: landscape) and (max-height: 520px)` (the same 520px the TV page already uses at `tv.css:1961`). A short window is cramped whatever it is a window of; tablets in landscape (768px tall and up) are a different question (open question 3).
2. Chrome budget: header 44px or less (about 11% of 390) and either sticky or reachable without a 5-screen swipe; sheet head 44px, grip 12px.
3. Use the width for a second column wherever the content is a stack of independent blocks: pair (words | actions), home (heading + hint | paste + Play), remote (title + seek + transport | speed + three buttons), title (art | Play + rating + text), settings (two columns). Keep one column for single-purpose pages (profiles, devices, security).
4. First-screen contract, measured per screen: pair shows Connect; home shows heading, paste, Play and one complete card; title shows Play; remote shows everything; menu shows three rows; settings shows three sections without scrolling.
5. Pictures are sized by height as well as width: the title art at most 36-45% of the sheet's height, or beside the text; sticky media never taller than a third of its scroller.
6. Sheets: `max-height: calc(100% - 8px)` of the fixed backdrop (not `86vh`), up to 680-780px wide, never a bottom column of 520px.
7. Edge-to-edge pages take their side padding from one variable: `max(16px, env(safe-area-inset-left), env(safe-area-inset-right))`.
8. A screen change resets its own scroll (remote, account and settings pages).
9. With the keyboard up the field's action sits beside the field (pair form already does in the draft); the visible height to design for is 170-210px.
10. Do not shrink touch targets in compaction: 44px stays 44px.
11. Test matrix: add 568x320, 800x360, 844x390, 932x430 to the phone walk; `p-land.mjs` and `p-draft.mjs` are ready-made first-screen probes.

## 3. Patterns (root causes)

- **R1. Fixed-ratio media in boxes that the height limits (F01, F04, F16).** The title art, the caption preview and the TV's compact banner are sized from the width (or a fixed em) and never from the height, so a short window gives them 74-89% of the box. Fix once: a height cap in the landscape rule, and a rule that sticky media is at most a third of its scroller.
- **R2. One scroll element reused across screens without a reset (F02, F07, partly F18).** The library, the remote and every account page share the document or one `.page-body` scroller; nobody resets it. A single helper on screen change fixes both.
- **R3. Independent, discontinuous thresholds instead of one rule (F06, F11, F21, F22).** The phone has max-height 700 and 740 and a width 380; the TV has 899/900 and 520/521 steps; nothing is keyed to orientation or aspect. Replace by one landscape rule keyed to height (principle 1) and continuous clamps on the TV.
- **R4. The layout assumes a centred 520px portrait column and ignores wide screens and side insets (F09, F15, and the cause of F03/F08 in landscape).** On the phone the column hides the missing insets by accident; the TV page's rail and corner button do not.
- **R5. The hand-held TV layout is a patched-down 10-foot layout (F10, F13, F16).** The em floor, the 56px rail and the 24-35px HUD targets come from the TV scale, not from a thumb; the one place where it was designed for a hand is the detail page's wrapping buttons.
- **R6. Phone sheets and pages are portrait objects (F05, F08, F25, F01).** A bottom column, 62px head and `86vh` assume 700px of height; in landscape they leave 250px of body.
