# H: Accessibility audit (WCAG 2.2 AA; TV with D-pad, phone, desktop keyboard)

2026-10-13, branch `ui/polish-system`, auditor H. Read-only on the product. Result: **30 findings: 0 P0, 2 P1, 15 P2, 13 P3.** Nothing locks anybody out; two things are clearly wrong in main flows (a page that does not take the focus, and white labels on blue and purple buttons).

## 1. Scope and method

Two roles, three ways in: the TV page (`/tv`: D-pad, remote, mouse), the phone remote (`/`: touch) and a desktop keyboard on either (the "any device as screen" and "phone as guest" cases). Checklist: `design:accessibility-review` (perceivable, operable, understandable, robust) at WCAG 2.2 AA. I walked the classic look (the default). The polish look shares markup, ARIA and the accent tokens (its `polish/tv.css` repeats the `--accent-ink` pairs), so the findings carry over; I did not walk it separately. Own server on port 8825 (in-memory data, library off), a guest profile seeded with progress, lists and ratings, plus sign-up and friend flows. Scripts and raw results are in my scratch folder `...\scratchpad\audit-H` (`axe-*.json`, `live-contrast-*.json`, `focus-probe-*.json`, `phone-keyboard.json`, `tv-keyboard.json`, `reflow*.json`, `targets-phone.json`, `placeholders.json`, `motion.json`, `shots/`). Line numbers are from the working tree at the time of the audit (`Controller.tsx` carries one extra import line, `polish/phone.css`, against `HEAD`, so its cites are one higher than in `HEAD`).

## 3. Patterns

1. **A layer does not own the focus or the accessibility tree** (H01, H03, H04). Only `Sheet` focuses in, traps and restores; `Page`, `Scanner` and the TV overlays do none of it, and nothing marks the page behind as hidden.
2. **One ink rule for six accents** (H02, H09, H18). `--accent-ink` covers the light three, not blue and purple; accent as text, pressed fills and disabled looks use the raw accent.
3. **ARIA roles without their contract, and state told twice** (H07, H08, H12, H13, H24). Roles are right and the names are good, but arrows, roving focus and one-source-of-truth for state are missing.
4. **Changes that happen are not announced** (H05, H06, H25; H22 for fields). Many messages are live regions, so the gaps are the search outcome and the TV's connection state.
5. **Hint and state colours are hard-coded, not tokens** (H10, H14, H18, H27). White at 18 %, the browser's placeholder grey, `opacity: .4`, the OFF track and the forced-colours case are each decided in place.
6. **The phone has no focus, target or motion design of its own** (H15, H16, H17, H19, H26). The TV has one; the phone leans on browser defaults and 36 to 40 px chrome.
7. **The TV HUD floats on a thin scrim and a short timer** (H11, H12, H23).
