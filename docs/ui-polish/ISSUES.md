### UI-001 Signed-in TV Settings is taller than the screen: first heading cut off

- **Where:** `tv-1280-signed-in/s13-account-panel.png`; classic `tv.css:114-122, 1465-1478`
- **Category:** TV layout & fit. **Severity:** P0 (audit: A01 P1; raised). **Scope:** both. **Status:** open (classic only; polish: 7e183fb).
- **Problem:** Signed-in TV Settings is 717 px (56.0 em) tall on a 720 px (56.25 em) screen: the LANGUAGE heading is cut at the top (y = -6) and the last row ends 9 px above the bottom edge. `.tv-screen` scrolls only under 900 px wide or 520 px high. - **Validate:** `pnpm ui:walk -- --scan` and compare `s13-account-panel` (first heading y >= 0, last row bottom <= 720 at 1280x720 and 1920x1080); signed-in case added to the Settings fit spec in `e2e/tv-screens.spec.ts`.
- **Note:** Polish scan (22:36): first heading y = 37, last line ends y = 708 of 720: fits with 12 px spare (not yet inside a 5 % safe margin).

- **Where:** `tv/Browse.tsx:509-514` (first focus, once), `:654-677`, `:902`; audit E Appendix A
- **Category:** TV flow. **Severity:** P0 (audit: E01 P1; raised). **Scope:** both. **Status:** open.

### UI-006 Phone account pages and the QR scanner never take focus (keyboard walks the library behind them)

- **Where:** `controller/AccountScreen.tsx:21-40` (`Page`), `Controller.tsx:545`, `controller/Scanner.tsx:114-121`, `PairScreen.tsx:143`; model: `Sheet.tsx:136-155`
- **Category:** Accessibility. **Severity:** P0 (audit: H01 P1; raised). **Scope:** both. **Status:** open.
- **Problem:** Account, Settings, Friends, Devices and Security pages (`Page`, `role=dialog aria-modal`) and the QR scanner never take focus. The page is mounted before the header, so focus stays on the opener and forward Tab walks the library hidden behind it; the page's own controls are reached only backwards with Shift+Tab. Any step inside, and Escape, drops focus on `<body>`, not on the opener. The scanner has no trap and its Close is the 3rd Tab stop. Also (H seen on the way): after any in-page click the phone's focus falls to `<body>`.
- **Why it feels unpolished:** Keyboard, switch and some screen-reader users work on a page whose focus they cannot see and lose their place on every step (WCAG 2.4.3, 2.4.7). The Sheet next door does all of this right.
- **Proposed fix:** One shared `useDialogFocus` (focus the dialog on mount, trap Tab, give focus back on close), lifted from `Sheet.tsx:136-155` and used by `Page` and `Scanner`.
- **Risk:** Medium: `Page` and the scanner are mounted in several places; the account layer sits inside `<div className="phone-ui layers">`; `useBack` listeners double-fire if the page around is told from an effect.
- **Validate:** Keyboard walk (14 forward Tabs with the account page open must stay inside; Escape returns focus to the opener); `e2e/accounts.spec.ts`, `e2e/handheld.spec.ts`.
- **Note:** Ran it: 14 forward Tabs landed behind the page; focus outside the dialog on 9 page screens (`phone-keyboard.json`). Not touched by 9fc7642 (which only scrolls the page body to top).
- **Sources:** H01, H §7 (E and U1)

### UI-008 Stylesheets assume Chromium about 88 and have no fallback for inset, aspect-ratio, :focus-visible, gap or min/max

- **Where:** classic `tv.css:9` (+33 more `inset`), 13 `aspect-ratio`, 72 `:focus-visible`, 189 flex `gap`, `tv.css:13` `--em`, 0 `@supports`; focus rules `tv.css:98-110`, lists dropped whole `tv.css:1390,1393`, `account.css:157`; `Browse.tsx:772`
- **Category:** Motion, performance & compatibility. **Severity:** P0 (audit: I02 P0*, E02 P1). **Scope:** both. **Status:** waiting on owner (browser floor).
- **Problem:** `inset` (34 uses, none preceded by top/right/bottom/left): `.tv { position: fixed; inset: 0 }` collapses on < 87 and every absolute `inset:0` layer with it: black screen. `aspect-ratio` (13 uses, 12 rules without a height or padding fallback): posters, stills and wide art collapse on < 88. `:focus-visible` (72 uses): no ring, scale or white fill anywhere on < 86 (E02: a D-pad UI with no visible focus is a lock-out; the debug panel probes it, `shared/debug.ts:98`, but `polish.test.ts:9-23` bans `@layer`, `:has()`, `:is()`, `text-wrap` and more, not `:focus-visible`). Flex `gap` (189 declarations): spacing collapses on < 84. `--em: max(min(...))` is invalid on < 79 and `font-size: var(--em)` falls back to 16 px. Logical shorthands (8 uses) lose their padding on < 87. `:is()` at classic `tv.css:590-591` carries the HUD's focus captions for icon-only buttons: a browser without `:is()` drops the whole rule, so those buttons show no caption on focus (H). `:has()` at `tv.css:1186` sits inside `@media (hover: hover)`, so TVs skip it. None reproduced for lack of an old engine.
- **Why it feels unpolished:** One missing feature turns a stated-target TV into a blank or unusable one, and there is no `@supports` to degrade gracefully.
