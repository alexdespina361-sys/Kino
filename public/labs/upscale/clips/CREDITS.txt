The free clips of the upscaler test page (/labs/upscale/): 45 seconds of two of the Blender Foundation's open films, video only, made
smaller for the test. Both films are licensed Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

tears-of-steel-480p-45s.mp4

Cut from the official downloads at download.blender.org with ffmpeg
(-ss <start> -t 45 -an, scale/crop as above, libx264 -preset slow -crf 22 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart).
