# ISSUE_LOG

Severity: **P0** breaks the app, or shows the cat to people who did not ask · **P1** a defect a viewer would see in normal use, or a leak · **P2** an edge case, or something that looks wrong at a closer look · **P3** a nit. "Found by" says how, so a reader can tell a measured defect from an opinion. Dates are 2026-10-20/21 (the branch was made in one night).
