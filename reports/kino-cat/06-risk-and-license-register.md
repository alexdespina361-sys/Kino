## Risks

Likelihood (L) and impact (I): low / medium / high. Status: open, mitigated, accepted.

| # | Risk | L | I | Mitigation in place | Residual / what to do | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | The cat reaches people who did not ask for it | low | high | four gates, all off by default: host `CAT_COMPANION`, role = the screen that plays, a per-device switch in Settings, URL override only for testing; e2e 1–2 prove nothing is fetched and no cat appears when a gate is shut | the host owner must not set `CAT_COMPANION=on` until they have looked at it; **nothing is deployed or enabled** | mitigated |
| R2 | A bug in the cat breaks the TV page | low | high | the cat is a lazy chunk behind `try/catch` (a failed chunk, mesh or context logs a warning and the page carries on: e2e 18–20); its canvas is `pointer-events: none` except a small hit box on the cat; it never takes focus; the soak checks 350 sessions × 600 s of bad afternoons for invariants | the independent review (05) looked for the rest; see ISSUE_LOG | mitigated |
| R3 | The cat covers something the viewer needs (focus ring, subtitles, controls, names under cards) | medium | medium | keep-out rectangles for words, buttons and the focus on the library; in a film the cat's layer is straight after the picture, under the subtitles, the controls and the toasts, so it cannot cover one (and is not chased off by one); the silhouette table (from the pixels of the drawn cat) tells the brain how much room it takes; `soft` words and `restSpot` for the names; soak invariant "never stands in a keep-out for more than 2.8 s" | the cat can still rest over a name when the shelf it is on has none to spare: 70 of 211 resting samples on the reviewer's worst seed at 1280×720, 0 of 159 and 0 of 161 on two others (05; ISSUE_LOG #9); a pause after a hop or at the seat next to the focused card is not name-aware (#10); the cat stays over a newly focused card for about 0.6 s (#37) | open (minor) |
| R4 | It costs too much on a weak screen (a Fire Stick, an old TV) | medium | high | budget 2 ms (desktop) / 4 ms (TV agent), a governor that moves detailed → lean → simple cat → none from *measured* cost (a one-pixel readback; see ISSUE_LOG #2), `prefers-reduced-motion`, quiet by default on a TV agent | **no real TV was measured.** First thing to do with a real TV: `?cat=debug` and read `quality`, `cost`, `fps` | open |
| R5 | The timing probe itself hurts (a synchronous readback, asked twice back to back 1.5 s after anything about the picture changed and then every 5 s) | low | low | measured on the real GPU: no hitch (worst frame gap 8.8 ms at 129 fps); on a software screen it is the point of it | on a driver where a readback is slow the governor would see that cost and step down, which is conservative | accepted |
| R6 | WebGL is missing, lost or too small on a device | medium | low | checks for WebGL1, uniform vector count and the mesh header; a lost context or a failed compile falls back to the simple cat; mesh fetch times out after 8 s | – | mitigated |
| R7 | Memory or listener leaks from turning it on and off | low | medium | everything the cat adds is removed on `stop()`; settings changes re-evaluate at once; 25 on/off cycles leave one cat, one canvas, a flat heap (05) | not run for hours in a real browser | mitigated |
| R8 | A licence problem: someone's art or code in the cat | low | high | the cat is generated by code, no assets, no new dependency, Furever not opened (above) | the owner's reel and the installer were not examined, so nothing was "inspired by" them beyond the high-level ideas in 01 | mitigated |
| R9 | Privacy: the cat sends data somewhere | low | high | it makes **no** network request except the lazy chunk and the mesh (same origin); its settings live in the device's own storage; `/api/features` returns only `{cat: boolean}`; no analytics or telemetry were added; the feedback system collects no cat data | – | mitigated |
| R10 | Accessibility: motion for people who asked for none; noise for screen readers | low | medium | `prefers-reduced-motion` = the cat only rests in one place; the canvas is `aria-hidden`; not in the tab order; the panel is reached from a corner button and is a normal menu for the remote and the keyboard | not tried with a screen reader | open (unverified) |
| R11 | The cat annoys (too busy, or asleep all the time) | medium | low | quiet on a TV by default; "quiet" and "no cat in films" switches; an unattended cat is asleep 43 % and walks 13 % of the time (ANIMATION_BACKLOG) | taste; the numbers are in `choose()` | accepted |
| R12 | A film is disturbed by the cat | low | high | in a film the cat sleeps in a bottom corner, in a layer straight after the picture and so under the controls, the subtitles and the toasts (a computed z-index of 0 against the captions' auto; a 40 s film with a line every 4 s: the cat never came or went); a device can switch it off for films; the cat never touches the player, plays nothing, never autoplays (e2e 5, 9–11) | not run through a real, long film with subtitles on a TV; the video's clock was not checked, only the player's state | mitigated |
| R13 | The kill switch does not kill | low | high | unset `CAT_COMPANION` → `/api/features` says `{cat:false}` → every screen's next load has no cat and does not fetch the chunk (e2e 2); `/api/health` shows `features.cat` | a screen that is already open keeps its cat until it reloads | mitigated |
| R14 | Large repository diff / merge friction | medium | low | 69 files, +12.3k lines and 2 deleted (30 commits: `src/client` 10.4k, `e2e` 0.7k, `scripts/cat` 1.1k, `src/server` 54), almost all new files in `src/client/cat/`, `scripts/cat/` and the reports; changes to existing files are small (00); `dist/client` is rebuilt in one separate last commit so a rebase can drop and redo it | – | accepted |
